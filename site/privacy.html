Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

What we collect

When you request a report, we receive the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) you enter. A VIN identifies a vehicle, not a person. We do not ask for or store your name, Tesla account credentials, or vehicle access of any kind.

Where the data comes from

Vehicle configuration, option and battery-health information is obtained from Tesla, Inc. through its official Fleet API under Tesla's developer terms. We request only read-only vehicle specification and pricing data. We never send commands to a vehicle and never access a vehicle's location or live state.

How we use and keep it

We cache report results by VIN so that a repeated request does not require a new query to Tesla. Cached reports are retained for up to 30 days and are shown only to users who request that VIN. Aggregate, non-identifying statistics (for example, counts of reports run) may be kept indefinitely.

Sharing

We do not sell or share report data with third parties. Service providers that host this site may process technical logs (such as IP addresses) solely to operate the service.

Your choices

To have a cached report for a VIN you own removed, contact hello@evbuildsheet.com with the VIN.

Not affiliated with Tesla

This service is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Tesla, Inc.
