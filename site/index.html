EV Build Sheet

The factory build sheet for any used EV, plus which software packages are active today and Tesla’s current battery-health estimate. Enter a VIN. Tesla vehicles supported first.

What a report shows

  • Trim, paint, wheels and interior as recorded at the time of sale
  • Factory option codes, including Autopilot and Full Self-Driving packages, and whether they are active
  • Tesla's current battery-health estimate (remaining usable capacity) when available
  • Production date and country of origin

Reports draw on vehicle configuration data provided through Tesla's official Fleet API. Lookups are read-only; no vehicle commands are issued and no vehicle is accessed.

Who it's for

Private buyers and small dealers evaluating a used Tesla listed by a dealer, auction or private seller, who want to confirm the seller's description against Tesla's own records.
